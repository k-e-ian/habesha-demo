IITona

The second round: share it, the Habesha way

First time eating Ethiopian? Our staff will guide you. Here's the short version.

One plate, together

Everything arrives on one big injera. Sharing from the same plate is the point.

Tear, scoop, eat

Tear a piece of injera with your right hand and use it to scoop the wot. No cutlery needed.

White or brown?

White injera is tangy and soft. Brown is nuttier and milder. Not sure? Ask for half and half.
