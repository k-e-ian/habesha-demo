Our menu is served like our coffee

The second round · Tona

Share it, the Habesha way

First time eating Ethiopian? Our staff will guide you. Here's the short version.

  1. One plate, togetherEverything arrives on one big injera. Sharing from the same plate is the point.
  2. Tear, scoop, eatTear a piece of injera with your right hand and use it to scoop the wot. No cutlery needed.
  3. White or brown injera?White is tangy and soft; brown is nuttier and milder. Not sure? Ask for half and half.
The third round · Baraka

Buna, the coffee ceremony

Baraka is the last cup, the blessing that sends you on your way.

Green beans are roasted at your table, ground by hand and brewed in the clay jebena, with frankincense and popcorn. The coffee is poured three times, each round a little lighter.
