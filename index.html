Share it, the Habesha way

First time eating Ethiopian? Here's the short version.

  1. One plate, togetherEverything arrives on one big injera. Sharing it is the point.
  2. Tear, scoop, eatTear a piece with your right hand and scoop the wot. No cutlery needed.
  3. White or brown injera?White is tangy and soft, brown is nuttier. Ask for half and half.

Buna, the coffee ceremony

The last cup, the blessing that sends you on your way.

Green beans are roasted at your table, ground by hand and brewed in the clay jebena, with frankincense and popcorn. Then the coffee is poured three times.
