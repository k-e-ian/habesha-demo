One injera. Everyone eats.

Everything arrives on one big round of injera and the table shares it. Our menu is poured like our coffee, in three rounds. Add dishes as you go and they land on your injera.

አቦል

Abol

The first round, the strongest cup. From the fire: tibs still sizzling when they reach you.

ቶና

Tona

The second round, for sharing. First time eating Ethiopian? It takes three steps.

  1. 1One plate, togetherEverything arrives on one injera. Sharing it is the point.
  2. 2Tear, scoop, eatTear a piece with your right hand and scoop the wot.
  3. 3White or brownWhite injera is tangy, brown is nuttier. Or ask for both.
በረካ

Baraka

The third round, the blessing. Buna, the coffee ceremony.

Green beans are roasted at your table, ground by hand and brewed in the clay jebena, with frankincense and popcorn. Then the coffee is poured three times.
