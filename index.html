Three rounds

Our menu is served like our coffee

A coffee ceremony is poured in three rounds: abol, tona and baraka. Our menu follows it. Tap + and each dish lands on your injera.

The first round · Abol

From the fire

Abol is the strongest cup, so this is our strongest plate: tibs still sizzling when it reaches you.

The second round · Tona

Share it, the Habesha way

First time eating Ethiopian? Here's the short version.

  1. One plate, togetherEverything arrives on one big injera. Sharing it is the point.
  2. Tear, scoop, eatTear a piece with your right hand and scoop the wot. No cutlery needed.
  3. White or brown?White injera is tangy and soft; brown is nuttier. Ask for half and half.
The third round · Baraka

Buna, the coffee ceremony

Baraka is the last cup, the blessing that sends you on your way.

Beans are roasted at your table, ground by hand and brewed in the clay jebena, with frankincense and popcorn. The coffee is poured three times.
